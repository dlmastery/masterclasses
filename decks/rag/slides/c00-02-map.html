Each chapter ends with interview questions.
This map is the whole course on one diagram. Step 1 is the offline lane: parse, chunk, embed and index, taught in chapters 2 and 3. Step 2 is the online lane that runs for every query: retrieve about 150 candidates with hybrid search, rerank to 20, assemble the context, call the model; that is chapter 4. Step 3 bends the line into a loop: an agent checks the evidence and searches again when it is not enough; chapters 5 and 6 cover routers, Self-RAG, CRAG, deep research and RL-trained search. Step 4 adds other stores: graphs in chapter 7, page images and memory in chapter 8. Step 5 puts evaluation, security and operations around every stage, in chapters 9 and 10. Analogy: the offline lane is a library that catalogues its books; the online lane is the librarian who answers one question. Likely question: "Where do I start when my RAG system gives bad answers?" Answer: measure recall of the first stage before you change the prompt; chapter 1 shows why.
