Every chapter: gotchas, 2026 papers, one production case

This is the whole course on one page. Start: a question goes in and a cited answer comes out; everything else fills the gap between them. Click 1, the offline lane on the left, read top to bottom: Docling parses the documents, we split them at headings, embed them with Gemini and store the vectors in FAISS or pgvector; this runs once per document change. Click 2, the online lane: the question drops into Retrieve, and the flow runs left to right. For each question, BM25 (keyword match) and dense search (embedding match) find candidates, RRF merges the two ranked lists, and a cross-encoder re-scores each question-chunk pair; Lab 4 builds the hybrid index in Qdrant. Chapter 5 has no lab; it adds build-ups to the Lab 4 code: query rewrites and contextual chunks. Click 3: when an LLM grades the evidence as too weak, the agentic loop rewrites the query and retrieves again. Click 4, the guard ring: an eval gate in CI, access control and injection tests, and managed RAG on Google Cloud decide whether the stack ships. Each lab uses the corpus that best shows its failure mode; the pipeline stays the same. Analogy: the offline lane stocks the library, the online lane is the librarian, the ring is the auditor. Likely question: "Why start with BM25?" Without a measured floor you cannot prove that an upgrade helps. Each lab's first slide gives its pinned install line; the next slide shows the setup and the API keys.
