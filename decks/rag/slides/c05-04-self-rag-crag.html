Pitfall: an uncalibrated LLM grader marks everything “relevant”. Then the corrective branch never fires. Check the grader against human labels first.
Two ways to check evidence. Self-RAG (Asai et al.) trains one model to write reflection tokens into its own output: Retrieve says whether to fetch, ISREL grades each passage, ISSUP checks that the sentence is supported, ISUSE scores usefulness. The model writes one continuation per passage and keeps the best one by a weighted critique score. CRAG (Yan et al.) leaves the generator alone and adds a T5-large evaluator of about 0.77B parameters: Correct refines the documents into knowledge strips, Incorrect rewrites the query into keywords and searches the web, Ambiguous does both. With the same Self-RAG-LLaMA2-7B generator, CRAG lifts PopQA from 52.8 to 59.8 and PubHealth from 39.0 to 75.6. Analogy: Self-RAG is a writer who learned to fact-check while writing; CRAG is an editor who checks the sources before the writer starts. Likely question: "Which one for a hosted API model?" CRAG, because you cannot fine-tune the hosted model to emit reflection tokens.
