Chapter 8 is Lab 7: Harbor Mutual claims investigations, where an investigator must see that two hail claims on Elm St belong to one case. The animation shows the three parts of the chapter: LightRAG reads three claim files and links Claim 2026-041, Claim 2026-057, adjuster Maria Lopez, Elm St and the SIU review rule; then two claim photos land in the same gemini-embedding-2 space as a text question; then the assistant recalls a past decision from raw turns with a reranker. The lede is the test for every technique in this chapter: a graph, an image index or a memory store must find evidence that plain chunks miss, or it is only extra ingestion cost. Analogy: an investigator's pin board links the files, the photo folder sits beside it, and the case log keeps what was said word for word. Likely question: "Should every RAG system get a knowledge graph?" No. The EffiRAG paper later in the chapter shows that the graph is worth it mainly to locate passages, and you answer from the source text. Next: step 1 of Lab 7 wires LightRAG to Gemini.
