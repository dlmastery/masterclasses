# step 1: imports, MODEL, take_question, router, route_on_category, retrieve_hr
# step 3 defines: answerer, verifier, gate (the HR chain, next slide)

it_agent = Agent(name="it_agent", model=MODEL,
                 instruction="Give a 2-line IT first-aid tip for: {question}")
def refuse():
    yield Event(message="I can only help with HR policy and IT questions.")

root_agent = Workflow(name="hr_helpdesk", edges=[
    ("START", take_question, router, route_on_category),
    (route_on_category, {"hr_policy": retrieve_hr,
                         "it_support": it_agent,
                         "out_of_scope": refuse}),
    (retrieve_hr, answerer, verifier, gate),
])

async def main():
    runner = InMemoryRunner(node=root_agent)
    for i, q in enumerate(["How many PTO days can I carry over into 2027?",
                           "Can you book me a pizza?"]):
        await runner.run_debug(q, session_id=f"s{i}")
if __name__ == "__main__":
    asyncio.run(main())

This is step 2 of Lab 5: lab5_helpdesk.py now turns the nodes from step 1 into a graph with three edge tuples. it_agent is a placeholder that step 4 replaces with it_triage, and the three HR-chain nodes answerer, verifier and gate are the subject of the next slide. Walk the bands: the first tuple is a chain from "START" through take_question and router to route_on_category; the dict tuple is a conditional edge map, and the key comes from Event(route=...); the third tuple chains retrieve_hr, answerer, verifier and gate, and Workflow validates the whole graph in model_post_init. The token shows the run: the pizza message takes the out_of_scope edge and stops at refuse, the PTO question runs the whole HR chain to gate. Analogy: the edges list is a railway timetable, and route_on_category is the switch operator who throws one lever per train. Likely question: "Why InMemoryRunner(node=...)?" Because a Workflow is a BaseNode, not a BaseAgent, so agent= is the wrong slot; agent= and node= are mutually exclusive, and agent-only code paths raise the TypeError on the card.
