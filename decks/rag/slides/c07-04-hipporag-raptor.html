HippoRAG 2: PageRank over a KG as memory

  1. Offline: OpenIE triples → phrase nodes, synonym edges (similarity > 0.8), passage nodes linked by "contains".
  2. Online: query → top-5 triples → LLM recognition filter → seeds: kept phrases + all passages (weight × 0.05).
  3. Personalized PageRank spreads activation from the seeds. Passages rank by score → reader.

RAPTOR (Stanford): retrieve over the collapsed tree, all levels at once; it beats top-down traversal. With GPT-4: +20% absolute on QuALITY. RAPTOR clusters chunks by similarity; GraphRAG clusters entities by connectivity.

HippoRAG models the hippocampus: the LLM is the cortex, the knowledge graph is the index, and Personalized PageRank is pattern completion. Watch the heat: the query picks triples, an LLM drops the bad ones, the kept phrases become seeds, and PageRank spreads activation along edges until passages two hops away light up. That is associative recall: the passage about Rockland County never matched the query words, but it wins through Montebello. RAPTOR takes the other road: it clusters chunks by embedding similarity, writes a summary per cluster and repeats, so long documents get a tree of summaries; search all levels at once. Then the metric trap: graph papers report LLM-judge win rates, HippoRAG 2 reports F1, and LightRAG goes from about 50% to 6.6 depending on the metric. Likely question: "Which number do I trust?" The one that matches your task; for factual QA, F1 against a strong dense baseline.
