lab1_ragas_refund.py (part 1)
import asyncio, math, os
from openai import AsyncOpenAI
from ragas.cache import DiskCacheBackend
from ragas.llms import llm_factory
from ragas.metrics.collections import ContextPrecision, ContextRecall, Faithfulness

# Gemini through its OpenAI-compatible endpoint
client = AsyncOpenAI(api_key=os.environ["GEMINI_API_KEY"],
                     base_url="https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/")
judge = llm_factory("gemini-3.8-flash", provider="openai", client=client,
                    max_tokens=4096,                       # default is 1024
                    cache=DiskCacheBackend(".ragas_cache"))

POLICY = [
    "Physical goods may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.",
    "Digital gift cards are non-refundable once the code has been revealed.",
    "Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.",
]
SAMPLES = [
    {"user_input": "Can I return headphones delivered 10 days ago?",
     "response": "Yes. Physical goods can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund.",
     "reference": "Yes, physical goods can be returned within 14 days of delivery.",
     "retrieved_contexts": [POLICY[0], POLICY[2]]},
    {"user_input": "I revealed my gift card code. Can I get a refund?",
     "response": "Yes, gift cards can be refunded within 30 days to your original card.",
     "reference": "No. Digital gift cards are non-refundable once the code is revealed.",
     "retrieved_contexts": [POLICY[2], POLICY[1]]},   # relevant chunk ranked 2nd
]
faith, cp, cr = Faithfulness(llm=judge), ContextPrecision(llm=judge), ContextRecall(llm=judge)

This is the run-it-Monday slide of Lab 8: pin ragas 0.4.3, openai 3.37.0 as a plain client, and diskcache, and use the ragas 0.4 collections API, because the legacy ragas.metrics plus evaluate() path is deprecated and goes away in v1.0. Walk the four bands: the judge talks to Gemini through its OpenAI-compatible base URL, because ragas routes a google-genai client to instructor.from_genai, and the ragas source itself warns about invalid safety settings on that path; then max_tokens goes to 4096, since the default 1024 is eaten by Gemini 3 thinking tokens; then the disk cache, so CI reruns make no judge calls until a prompt or an input changes; and last the two ShopCo rows, where row 2 invents a 30-day gift-card refund and the right chunk sits at rank 2. Also replace gemini-2.0-flash from the ragas docstrings: that model is shut down. Analogy: the judge is a lab instrument, and this slide is the calibration bench before any measurement. Likely question: "Why not call ragas evaluate() on a dataset?" The collections metrics are scored per row with ascore, which the next slide shows, and that also lets us see NaN rows instead of hiding them.
