Rewrite, contextualise or read it all: fix the queries that miss

Some misses are the query's fault, some are the chunk's, some are the window's.

Chapter 5 keeps the Northwind Outfitters support assistant and the Chapter 4 hybrid_search(), and fixes the queries that still miss. Each miss has an owner. q11 asks two things in one sentence, so the query is at fault: one Gemini structured call writes paraphrases, a hypothetical answer and sub-questions. q06 asks about the tent warranty, but chunk c07 never says "tent", so the chunk is at fault: Claude Haiku 5.5 writes the missing context once at ingest, with prompt caching. Some answers need the whole policy page, so the window is at fault: parent-child retrieval searches small children and returns the full parent. Every change is scored with the same Chapter 1 evaluate() harness, and two paper slides show when to skip a rewrite and when to move the work to index time. Analogy: a librarian can rephrase your question, label the books better, or hand you the whole shelf, and each fix has a different cost. Likely question: "With a 1M-token window, why not read it all?" The last slide answers it: 2026 work selects evidence inside the long context too. Next: Build 1 fixes q11.
