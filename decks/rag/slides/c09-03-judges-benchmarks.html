Step 1: an LLM judge is useful because strong judges agree with humans over 80% of the time, which is the level humans reach with each other. The same paper names three biases: position, verbosity and self-enhancement, so swap the answer order and use a judge from another model family. Step 2: ARES trains small judges on synthetic data and uses Prediction-Powered Inference; watch the confidence band get narrow as human labels come in. Step 3: the benchmark line moves from multi-hop QA to zero-shot retrieval to real RAG pipelines and agentic browsing. Step 4: on FRAMES, multi-step retrieval lifts accuracy from 0.40 to 0.66. Step 5: public sets leak into pretraining, so your private golden set is the number you trust. Analogy: a public exam paper that circulated before the exam. Likely question: "How big must the golden set be?" Start with 100 to 500 items and grow it from failed production traces.
