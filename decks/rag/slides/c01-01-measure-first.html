Measure first: a labelled set, four metrics and a BM25 floor

You cannot claim that hybrid search or reranking helps until you can print one table.

kb.py · 18 chunks · 15 labelled queries evalkit.py · recall@k · hit@k · MRR · nDCG floor run + BM25 · bm25s 0.3.15 every later lab calls evaluate() Python 3.12
This chapter builds the measuring stick before any retriever: the Northwind Outfitters support corpus of 18 policy chunks in 4 documents, 15 labelled support queries, and evalkit.py with recall@k, hit@k, MRR and nDCG. The animation is the whole chapter in one picture: the 15 queries run through evaluate(), a floor run that ignores the query fills the grey row with recall@5 = 0.333, and a BM25 baseline with bm25s 0.3.15 fills the green row. The floor numbers were executed; the BM25 row is illustrative until you run it. Analogy: you weigh yourself before the diet starts, or you can never say the diet worked. Likely question: "Why not go straight to embeddings?" Because without this table you cannot tell whether dense, hybrid or a reranker helped, and every later lab in the deck plugs into the same evaluate() call.
