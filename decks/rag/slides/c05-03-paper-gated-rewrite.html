Three papers from the last three months say that query rewriting is not free and does not always help. SAFE-QE counted 57.5 unsupported terms per query for HyDE against 4.3 for its filtered method at equal nDCG@10, which is why Build 1 sends the HyDE passage to the dense side only. Better Together shows that on a strong baseline one rewriter is at best on par, a union of rewriters gives +12.5 HIT@10, and a score gate keeps +4.3 of that while it rewrites fewer than 40% of queries; the rewritten pool is always reranked against the original question. Snap's SMART paper shows the same gate in production: about 10% of users take the LLM path, LLM cost falls by 90% and ad conversion rises 27.6% in a two-week A/B test. Analogy: a triage nurse sends only the unclear cases to the specialist. Likely question: "What is the gate signal?" Use a cheap first-pass signal such as the top-1 reranker score, and tune tau on your labelled set, not on the test queries.
