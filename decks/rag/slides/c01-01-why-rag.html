Why RAG

Models need knowledge they were never trained on.

  • Frozen. Knowledge stops at the training cutoff. An update needs a retrain.
  • No source. The model cannot cite, so you cannot audit a hallucination.
  • No private data. Tickets, contracts and inventory were never in training.
  • No access control. You cannot filter weights per user.

The RAG answer. Keep knowledge in an external index that you can swap and filter by permission. At query time, retrieve the top-k passages. Condition the answer on them.

Update = re-index, not retrain.

Start with the four limits of a model that answers only from its weights. Press once: the four risk tags appear around the model. Press again: the external index plugs in, and each risk becomes a gain. The analogy is an open-book exam: the student still needs to reason, but the facts come from the book, and you can swap the book. Stress the last point: an update is a re-index, not a retrain. Likely question: "Why not fine-tune the documents into the model?" Answer: fine-tuning changes behaviour and style, but it gives no citations, no per-user filter and no cheap update. The interview slide at the end of this chapter covers this trade-off.
