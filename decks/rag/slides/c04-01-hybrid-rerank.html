Hybrid search and reranking: two lists for recall, one model to order

Dense fixes paraphrases, BM25 fixes exact codes, a cross-encoder fixes the order.

Chapter 4 is Lab 4: we upgrade the retrieval of the Northwind Outfitters support assistant and score every step with the same Chapter 1 harness on the same 15 queries. The order is BM25, then dense BGE-small, then hybrid fused by reciprocal rank, then the same fusion inside Qdrant, then a cross-encoder over the top 20, and finally Cohere Rerank v5 with relevant spans as free context compression. The animation uses query q13, "who pays customs fees on orders to Canada?", with illustrative ranks: BM25 misses the gold chunk c18 because it says "import duties", dense ranks it first, RRF adds 1/(60 + rank) from both lists and puts c18 at rank 4, and the cross-encoder moves it to rank 1. Analogy: two scouts each bring a shortlist, and a judge who reads every candidate decides the order. Likely question: "If the reranker fixes the order, why fuse at all?" Because a reranker can only reorder what the first stage found; recall comes from the two lists, order comes from the model.
