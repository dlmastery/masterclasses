Start with the question on the slide. A closed-book model must guess, because last week's policy change is not in its weights. A RAG model finds the policy page, answers from it and cites it. Analogy: RAG is an open-book exam; the student still needs to know the subject, but checks the facts in the book and shows the page. The animation shows the 2020 pattern first (one retrieval call), then the 2026 pattern (an agent that thinks, searches, reads and searches again before it answers). Step 1 shows three numbers: the 21M-passage index of Lewis et al. 2020, Anthropic's drop in top-20 failures from 5.7% to 1.9%, and BrowseComp, where GPT-4o with browsing gets 1.9% and Deep Research gets 51.5%. Likely question: "Is RAG dead now that models have 1M-token windows?" Say no, and that chapter 4 gives the measurements (lost in the middle, context rot) and the decision rule.
