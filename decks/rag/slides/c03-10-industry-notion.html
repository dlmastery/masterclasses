Blog Two years of vector search at Notion (2026-02-19)
Notion's Q&A search is the clearest public story of what vector search costs after launch. Steps 0 and 1: it launched in November 2023 on a pod cluster sharded by workspace ID, added new index "generations" instead of re-sharding, and in May 2024 moved to serverless storage and compute, which cut cost 50% from the peak. Step 2: the move to turbopuffer was also a full re-index and an embedding-model upgrade, with 60% lower search spend and p50 latency down from 70-100 ms to 50-70 ms. Step 3: the Page State project stores an xxHash64 for each span's text and for its metadata, so only changed text is re-embedded and a metadata change is a PATCH, which cut data volume 70%. Step 4: moving from Spark to Ray, with Ray Serve hosting the query embedder, is reported as a 90+ percent cut in embedding infrastructure cost and is still rolling out. Analogy: a librarian who re-files only the pages that changed, and only re-labels a shelf when the label changed. Likely question: "Why hash metadata separately?" Because a permission or title change must reach the index, but it does not change the vector. Our Lab 3 build hashes only the text, so a metadata hash is the next step.
