Chapter 11 is Lab 10: we ship the GlobalCorp people-team assistant on managed services. The HR handbook goes into a RAG Engine corpus, the EU benefits handbook goes into a Gemini File Search store with region metadata and byte-accurate citations, and a RedisVL semantic cache answers repeated questions per region and policy version. The waterfall is the whole chapter: every request has a 3,000 ms budget, a miss pays for cache lookup, retrieval, rerank and the LLM, and a hit is one short cache bar; the US request misses on purpose because the EU answer is scoped to region EU. Analogy: a restaurant kitchen keeps the popular dish ready, but it never serves the vegetarian table from the meat tray. Likely question: "Why not cache everything?" Because only deterministic, non-personal, policy-scoped answers are safe to reuse, and we close the chapter with that rule in the Monday checklist.
