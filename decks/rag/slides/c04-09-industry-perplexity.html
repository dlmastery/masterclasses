Blog Architecting and evaluating an AI-first search API (2025-09-25) Case Vespa: Perplexity
Perplexity's search API is the same shape as our Northwind pipeline at a much larger scale: 200 million queries a day over more than 200 billion URLs, served on Vespa. Step 1 is indexing: an ML model decides whether and when to index each URL, LLM-scored parsing rules improve in a loop, and documents are split into sub-document units. Steps 2 and 3 are the query path: hybrid lexical plus semantic retrieval for recall, prefilters, lexical and embedding scorers, then cross-encoders at document and sub-document level, and the LLM reads spans, which is exactly what Cohere relevant_spans gave us in Lab 4 step 4. The latency and quality numbers in step 4 are Perplexity's own measurements, so say "vendor claim" out loud. Analogy: a wide net first, then finer and finer sieves, and only the best grains reach the cook. Likely question: "Why not rerank everything with the cross-encoder?" Because it scores each query-document pair, so cost grows with the candidate count; cheap scorers cut the list first.
