Shopify Sidekick is the merchant assistant inside the Shopify admin, and its team wrote down how they made the LLM judge trustworthy. Step 1: the ground truth comes from sampled real merchant conversations, each labelled by at least three experts, and a user simulator replays real goals against candidate systems before launch. Step 2: the judge started at a Cohen's kappa of 0.02, which is chance level, and reached 0.61, while two humans agree at only 0.69, so the human ceiling is the number to quote next to the judge. Step 3 is their swap test: put a human in the judge's seat at random, and if reviewers cannot tell which one is the judge, trust it. Analogy: a blind taste test for graders. Likely question: "Why not demand kappa 1.0?" Because humans themselves only reach 0.69 here; our Lab 8 build-up gets 1.00 on a tiny 6-claim set, which is a sign to grow the set, not a target for production.
