Best practices

Measure-first rules: real queries, span labels, per-query losses

Do

  • Build the set from real tickets or search logs. 15 queries teach; 100-300 decide.
  • Label evidence spans: mark every chunk that holds the answer. 2610.31606
  • Run the floor retriever first. A near-floor score means an ID bug.
  • Report recall@k over all gold next to hit@k. 2610.26951
  • Print the per-query "got worse" list next to every mean. Fusion hurts 8-21% of queries (2610.30612).
  • Cut MRR at 10. Compare ranx with "mrr@10", not "mrr".
  • Assert non-empty gold sets at load time. Else ZeroDivisionError.

Don't

  • Do not tune parameters on the test queries.
  • Do not compare systems on different labelled sets. Labels move scores.
  • Do not accept a score without its miss list.

# compare.py -- per-query losses between two retrievers (stdlib + evalkit)
import csv
from evalkit import ndcg_at_k
from kb import QUERIES

def per_query(search) -> dict[str, float]:
    return {qid: ndcg_at_k(search(t), gold, 5) for qid, (t, gold) in QUERIES.items()}

def got_worse(base, cand, eps: float = 1e-9) -> list[str]:
    a, b = per_query(base), per_query(cand)
    with open("per_query.csv", "w", newline="") as f:
        w = csv.writer(f)
        w.writerow(["qid", "base_ndcg5", "cand_ndcg5"])
        w.writerows((q, round(a[q], 3), round(b[q], 3)) for q in a)
    return [q for q in a if b[q] < a[q] - eps]

assert all(gold for _, gold in QUERIES.values()), "empty gold set"
These rules turn the Lab 1 harness into a habit. Steps 1 and 2: real queries, span-level labels, the floor run first, recall next to hit, and never a mean without the miss list; each rule has a reason from Lab 1 or a paper from this month. Step 3 to 5: compare.py scores both retrievers per query, writes a CSV, and returns the queries where the candidate is lower; in the illustrative chart the hybrid wins on the mean but two of fifteen queries got worse, which is the Fusion Tax pattern. Analogy: a class average can rise while two students fail, and those two are the support tickets that reach your manager. A likely question is "Why 100 to 300 queries?": with 15 queries one flip moves the score 6.7 points, so small wins are noise.
