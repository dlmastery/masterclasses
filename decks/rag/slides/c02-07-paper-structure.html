arXiv 2610.07091 2026-10-05

Smart Content Ingestion for Generative AI Workloads

A deterministic structure-aware parent-child chunker: hit@1 68.6%, hit@10 92.8%, MRR 0.77 over 25,050 generated questions.
Principle: "never mutate what you measure". Make the parent a policy section.
Three October papers agree on one point: the chunk boundary and the heading text matter more than clever extras. Step 1: the placebo-controlled ablation shows that real heading paths beat LLM-written chunk context, and that shuffled headings from other documents do not help, so the gain is the content of the prefix and not extra tokens; routing to a section first made results worse. Step 2: StrucChunk builds a Part, Chapter, Section tree for statutes and adds a breadcrumb like "CrPC 1973, Chapter XII, Section 167", and Recall@5 rose from 0.557 to 0.804 with 88% of the gain from chunking alone. Analogy: a fixed saw cuts a contract every 30 centimetres; a clerk cuts it at every clause and writes the clause number on each piece. Step 3 and 4: a production ingestion system with a structure-aware parent-child chunker reached hit@1 68.6% and hit@10 92.8%, and for Northwind Mutual the change is the one Lab 2 already made: split at headings and embed contextualize() output. Likely question: "Should I always add hybrid search too?" Not blindly: on GDPR hybrid hurt by 0.040, so measure per corpus. Next: Uber Genie shows the same lesson in production.
