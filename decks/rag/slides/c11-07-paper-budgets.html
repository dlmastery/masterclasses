Retrieval Augmented Generation Compositionality (LASAGNA)

450 configurations, 8 benchmarks, 5K to 200K docs. Three retrieval techniques predicted +47% together and delivered +17%. Better retrieval can hurt faithfulness. The 5K winner is not the 200K winner.
Re-run each change in the full stack at production corpus size. Track retrieval and faithfulness apart.

Three recent papers turn the GlobalCorp 3-second budget from a wish into an engineering rule. SAGE learns how many chunks each query needs, with no LLM call: under a 5-second P95 SLO it met the target 95 percent of the time against 30 percent for a static k of 20, at a cost of 2 points of exact match, and the Top-K paper shows the same idea for hybrid fusion. LASAGNA from IBM is the warning for the whole course: three techniques that each looked good alone predicted plus 47 percent together but delivered plus 17, and the best design at 5K documents lost at 200K. CacheSmith matters only if you serve your own model: reuse cached KV blocks when quality holds, because naive reuse drops attention across chunks. Analogy: three supplements that each help do not add up to triple health. Likely question: "Can I tune on a 5K sample and ship at 200K?" No; LASAGNA says re-run the full stack at production size.
