gemini_deep_research.py · Interactions API

import time
from google import genai
client = genai.Client()
interaction = client.interactions.create(
    input="Research the history of Google TPUs.",
    agent="deep-research-preview-04-2026",
    background=True)            # a long job: poll it
while interaction.status not in ("completed", "failed"):
    time.sleep(10)
    interaction = client.interactions.get(interaction.id)
print(interaction.steps[-1].content[0].text)  # or .error
Walk the canonical loop top to bottom. Step 1: the planner writes a research brief and splits it into sub-questions; Gemini lets the user edit this plan. Step 2: one researcher sub-agent per sub-question runs search, read, note in its own context window, in parallel; Anthropic's Opus 4 lead with Sonnet 4 sub-agents beat single-agent Opus 4 by 90.2% on an internal eval. Step 3: each lane compresses its notes and keeps the URLs; "the essence of search is compression", and token use alone explains 80% of BrowseComp variance. Step 4: a supervisor looks for gaps and spawns another lane; this is why multi-agent costs about 15x the tokens of chat. Step 5: the writer produces a report with inline citations, then the hosted API returns it from a background job that you poll. Analogy: an editor who sends four reporters out, reads their short memos, sends one more, then writes the story. Likely question: "Can I still call o3-deep-research?"
