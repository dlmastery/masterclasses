Blog Uber: Enhanced Agentic-RAG (2025-05-29)
Uber's Genie answers policy questions in Slack, and the team found that most of the quality was won or lost at ingestion. Step 1, offline: a custom Google Docs loader replaces the PDF loader, an LLM turns tables and bullets into markdown, table chunks are tagged so they are never split, and an LLM writes a summary, FAQs and keywords for each document into an offline feature store. Step 2, online: a Query Optimizer rewrites and splits the question, a Source Identifier picks the likely policy docs, vector plus BM25 search runs over the enriched metadata, and a Post-Processor removes duplicates and puts chunks back in source order before the answer LLM replies. Step 3: acceptable answers rose 27% and incorrect advice fell 60%, both relative, measured on 100+ SME queries with an LLM judge, and experiment turnaround went from weeks to minutes. Analogy: they stopped photocopying the policy binder and asked for the original Word file. Likely question: "What if I only have PDFs?"
