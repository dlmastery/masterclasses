Research agents, RL search and MCP

Deep research agents spend many tokens on many searches to answer one hard question.

ADK agentic RAG Deep research architecture RL-trained search agents MCP as the retrieval interface

“browsing alone is not sufficient—models must also be capable of reasoning strategically, identifying relevant search paths, and interpreting retrieved content.”

Paper BrowseComp: A Simple Yet Challenging Benchmark for Browsing Agents (arXiv 2504.12516)
BrowseComp has 1,266 questions. Each answer is hard to find but easy to check, so the score is exact. Press through the bars: GPT-4o alone gets 0.6%, browsing lifts it only to 1.9%, the reasoning model o1 gets 9.9%, and Deep Research gets 51.5%. The dashed line is the human baseline: trainers solved 29.2% and gave up on 70.8% after two hours. Analogy: a detective case where every clue leads to the next clue; a search box alone does not solve it, the plan does. This chapter shows how to build that plan: ADK workflow agents, the deep-research architecture, RL-trained searchers and MCP as the standard retrieval port. Likely question: "Is Deep Research better than people?" On this benchmark and with a two-hour human budget, yes; the benchmark was made so that people find the answers hard to locate.
