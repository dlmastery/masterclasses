Glean is an enterprise search and assistant company, and its August 2026 post compares its agent with Claude Cowork on more than 180 synthetic tasks from its own data; every number here is a vendor claim, so say so. Step 1 and 2: the cost per task fell from 2.98 to 0.58 dollars and tokens from 4.4 million to 1.3 million, and graders preferred Glean 78 percent of the time. Step 3: the reason is routing, because their Pareto study of 37 configurations found no single best model: Claude Opus 5 at high effort scored 67 at 2.96 dollars, GPT-5.6 Luna scored 55 at 8 cents. Step 4: Waldo, a small Nemotron 3 Nano search model, plans the searches and hands the evidence to the frontier model in about 250 milliseconds per call against about 3 seconds. Analogy: a librarian finds the books before the expert reads them. Likely question: "Should GlobalCorp route models?" Measure cost per successful task first, including retries; route only when the labelled set shows that a cheaper model passes. Next: the closing checklist, one rule per lab.
