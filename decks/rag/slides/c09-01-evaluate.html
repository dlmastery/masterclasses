Chapter 9 is Lab 8: the ShopCo refund assistant gets a release gate, so that no pull request can quietly make it worse. The animation is the whole chapter in one loop: three separate gauges for retrieval recall, grounding and canary abstention, each with its own baseline tick, feed one CI check, and when recall dips below its tick the check turns red and the merge is blocked. The lede is the design rule: one blended "RAG score" cannot tell you whether the retriever team, the prompt team or the model choice caused a drop, so we keep the metrics split. Analogy: a car dashboard has a fuel gauge and an oil light, not one "car health" number. Likely question: "Why not just trust ragas numbers?" Because the judge is itself a model you ship, so right after the CI step we calibrate it against human labels before it gates anything. Next: set up ragas with a Gemini judge for the two ShopCo rows.
