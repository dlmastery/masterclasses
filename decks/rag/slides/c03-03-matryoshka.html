Matryoshka dimensions and quantization shrink a 123 GB index to under 1 GB

Matryoshka Representation Learning

Train the encoder so that every prefix (the first 64, 128, 256 … dims) is itself a good embedding. Sum the loss over the nested sizes.

“up to 14x smaller embedding size … at the same level of accuracy”

Paper Kusupati et al., NeurIPS 2022

Re-normalize after you truncate (OpenAI docs). If you do not, cosine scores are wrong. Never compare truncated vectors with full vectors.
Funnel retrieval: search with short or binary vectors. Re-score the candidates with the full vectors.
The bar is one 3072-dimension embedding; each cell stands for 32 dimensions, and the counter is the size of an index of 10 million chunks. MRL trains the model so the first 768 dimensions already work as an embedding, so you cut the tail and go from about 123 GB to 31 GB. Then quantization: int8 gives about 7.7 GB, and one bit per dimension gives 96 bytes per vector, about 0.96 GB, 128 times smaller. Analogy: nested Russian dolls; each smaller doll is a complete doll, not a broken piece of the big one. Likely question: "Does binary not destroy accuracy?" Answer: use it only for the first pass, then re-score about 100 candidates with the full vectors, which is the funnel in step 4.
