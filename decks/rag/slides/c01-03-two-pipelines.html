Every RAG system, from a weekend demo to a managed service, has these two pipelines. Step 1 is the offline lane: parse, chunk, optionally add context, embed, and write three indexes. Step 2 is the online lane that runs on every query: rewrite, hybrid retrieve, rerank, then build the prompt for the LLM. Step 3 is the funnel: one million documents, 150 hybrid candidates, 20 after the reranker, 5 in the prompt. Step 4 names what each band decides: recall at the first stage, precision at the reranker, use at context assembly. Analogy: a library; the catalogue is built at night, and each visitor gets a librarian who narrows a million books to the five on the desk. Likely question: "Where do I start when answers are wrong?" Start at the top of the funnel: measure recall@k, because nothing downstream can bring back a chunk that the first stage dropped.
