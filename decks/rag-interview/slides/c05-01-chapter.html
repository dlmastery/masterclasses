Senior interviews end here: how do you know it works, and how do you keep it safe and fast? The animation shows two gates on the request path: the ACL and injection shield, and the p95 latency budget. The eval gate sits above the path with a dashed border, because it runs in CI once per release, not on every request. Watch the request that carries a poisoned chunk: it stops at the shield and never reaches the model. An analogy: an airport checks the passport and the boarding time of every passenger, and inspects the aircraft once before each flight. A likely question: "Which gate do teams forget?" Usually the eval gate, because the judge itself is never calibrated. The tag colours match the gates: red for security, green for evaluation, amber for latency. Three systems run through the chapter: the HelpDesk triage RAG that ships weekly behind an eval gate in CI, the Acme claims KB with per-group ACLs, and a 50M-document enterprise deployment.
