Foundations and ingestion

Most RAG failures start before the first query: in parsing, chunking and updates. You build ingest_acme.py, an incremental Docling ingest.

When RAG Chunking Incremental ingest
This chapter covers the screening questions that open most RAG interviews. Interviewers use them to see whether the candidate measures retrieval before tuning prompts. The scenario is the Acme Insurance claims knowledge base: policy PDFs, an adjuster manual and a support FAQ that change every week. Click 1 is the first run: the PDF splits into five chunks and all five are embedded. Click 2 is the second run after an edit to section 3: each chunk id is a sha256 of the model name and the chunk text, so four ids are unchanged and reused, and only chunk 3 is embedded. Analogy: you reprint one page of the binder, not the whole binder. Likely student question: "Why care about updates in an interview?" Because the day-2 problem, weekly changes and deletes, is where most designs fail. Hand-off: the first drill asks when RAG is the right tool at all.
