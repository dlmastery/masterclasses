This is the payouts flow from the durable-resume lab: kyc_lookup, then analyst_review (a human pause), then pay. Setup: SqliteSessionService("payouts.db") and App(resumability_config=ResumabilityConfig(is_resumable=True)) on google-adk 2.13.0. Demo it with two shell commands: python -m payouts.app start "ACME-GmbH" (process 1 pauses and exits), then python -m payouts.app resume yes (process 2 reads payouts.db). Click 1: kyc_lookup runs once; its output is saved as an event, a row in the session store. Click 2: analyst_review yields RequestInput and process 1 exits; the pause is data in SQLite. Click 3: process 2 opens the same session and fast-forwards kyc_lookup from its event without running it. analyst_review gets the answer. pay calls the gateway, but the process dies before pay's event is saved. Click 4: process 3 replays kyc_lookup and analyst_review, but pay has no saved event, so it runs again. That is at-least-once, and ADK's own docstring says the "tool call to resume needs to be idempotent". Only the step that was in flight at the crash repeats, so that is the one that needs an idempotency key. Likely question: "Does is_resumable=True switch resume on?" No. Replay works inside one invocation anyway; the flag adds cross-process durability and checkpoint events. Analogy: a bank teller who replays the receipt book after a power cut must not pay a cheque twice. The chapter uses this payouts flow for retry and resume, then a refund_approval flow for the human pause.
