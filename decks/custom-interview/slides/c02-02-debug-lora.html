This is the concrete script for the whole chapter, so show it first. Show the script and the first two output lines, and ask the room why a run that trains still barely moves. Step 1: without target_modules, PEFT uses the qwen3 default of q_proj and v_proj, so only 2 of 7 linear modules in each of the 28 layers adapt: 2,293,760 parameters instead of 10,092,544. Step 2: 2e-5 is the SFTConfig default for full fine-tuning; LoRA needs about ten times that, so use 2e-4. Step 3 is different: a string model id loads in float32 unless model_init_kwargs sets the dtype. That doubles the weight memory, from 1.2 GB to 2.4 GB, and slows the steps, but it does not lower quality. On a T4, which has no bf16, use float16 and fp16=True. The output lines are deterministic: the counts come from r times in plus out per module, and the dtype line comes from the print on line 16. In the grid, q and v are trained in both runs; the five green modules are the ones all-linear adds. Analogy: you hired seven painters, sent two of them to work, and gave them a brush one tenth of the right size. A likely follow-up: how do you catch this in CI?
