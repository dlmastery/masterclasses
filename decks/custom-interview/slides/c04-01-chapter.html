Data-centric AI: labels, leakage and synthesis

Interviewers ask about the data first. You will audit labels, drift, contamination and splits.

This chapter has three question slides, one whiteboard and one debug drill, all about the data, not the model. Click through the pipeline one gate at a time. The contract is the schema plus the allowed values for each column, so it rejects a bad row at the door. Dedup removes exact and near-duplicate rows inside the set. The label audit measures rater agreement and sends suspect labels to a review queue. The split puts rows into train and test by customer and by time. The leak check then compares train against test and stops a test row that is a near-twin of a train row. Analogy: a data pipeline is a customs line, and every gate stops one kind of problem. A likely question: "Why check for leakage after dedup?" Because dedup inside one set does not compare train against test. First question: two raters agree 92% of the time; is that good?
