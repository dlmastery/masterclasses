This chapter is about one habit: no eval number without n, a confidence interval, a slice and a version. A slice is a subgroup such as Spanish health claims; a version is the model plus the hash of the eval file. Click 1 shows the refund-agent lab: v1 scores 73.5% and v2 78.7% on the same 400 golden cases, and the two per-model intervals overlap, so it looks like a tie. Click 2 pairs item i of v1 with item i of v2. Discordant items are the items where exactly one model is right; concordant items cancel out in the delta, so only the discordant ones carry signal. Click 3 shows the interval of the delta: unpaired it is [-0.7, +11.0] points and crosses zero; paired it is [+0.5, +10.2] and does not. Analogy: to test a new running shoe, you time the same runners in both shoes, not two random crowds. Likely question: "Why not just collect more items?" You can, but pairing is free, and a power analysis tells you how many you need before you build the set.
