Blog Shopify: Building production-ready agentic systems
Shopify's Sidekick team (engineering post of 2025-08-26) published rough tool-count bands. Step 1: under 20 tools the boundaries between tools are clear; from 20 to 50 they blur; past 50 the model finds many ways to do the same task. Step 2: the Acme refund desk sits at 3 tools; Vercel's d0 v1 sat at 17, near the edge of the clear band, and still got faster with 2. Step 3: Shopify's fix was not a bigger system prompt: they return just-in-time instructions with the tool results, so guidance arrives where it is needed and the cached system prompt stays stable. Step 4: the lesson for Acme: keep the three tools and add one only when an eval shows a measured gain. Analogy: a toolbox with labelled drawers works until two drawers say "fasteners". Likely question: "What if I really need 60 tools?" Then split them behind skills or a toolset filter so the model sees only the ones a task needs, which Chapters 6 and 7 cover. Chapter 2 moves to the Acme claims desk: structured output, state and sessions.
