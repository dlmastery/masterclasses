Step 6 closes Lab 7: the 500 dollar rule that the refund_agent instruction only asks for becomes code. This slide shows the old way first. A before_tool_callback on the refunds root agent guards that one agent; its signature is positional, tool, args and tool_context, and the body here is a one-line stub that blocks amounts over 500. But refunds has two sub-agents, partner_refunds and chargebacks, and both call issue_refund with no callback, so a partner-store refund of 640 dollars on A-200 goes straight through. You could copy the callback onto every agent, and the next agent someone adds will miss it. Think of a lock on one office door while the side doors stay open. A likely question: "Is the callback wrong?" No, it works for its own agent; the problem is scope, and the next slide moves the rule to a plugin on the App.
