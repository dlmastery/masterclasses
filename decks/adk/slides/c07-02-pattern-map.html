The tree is the decision flow from the Google Cloud Architecture Center and Anthropic, in one picture. Ask one question at each box and stop at the first "yes". Press three times to send three real scenarios down the tree: the refund bot has fixed steps, so it is a chain, and its refund call gets a HITL overlay; the research assistant cannot predict its subtasks, so it is orchestrator-workers; the nightly ETL job is a plain chain. The last press shows that four catalogs (Ng, Anthropic, Gulli, Google Cloud) use different words for the same shapes, and each row ends in one ADK 2.0 primitive. Analogy: four field guides give the same bird four names; the bird is the same. Likely question: "Where is ReAct?" It is the "agent node" leaf at the bottom of the tree, bounded by max_llm_calls, which is the most expensive choice and the last resort before a swarm.
