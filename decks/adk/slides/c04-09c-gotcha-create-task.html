A child started with create_task fails without a sound

SymptomA child fails, and nothing reports it. Causeasyncio.create_task: "errors are silently swallowed". agents/context.py:453-456 FixAwait the child directly, or through asyncio.gather.
expense_audit/agent.py · orchestrate, excerpt
# BAD: a failing child is never reported
asyncio.create_task(ctx.run_node(auditor, node_input=l))

# GOOD: await directly or through gather; errors raise
raw = await asyncio.gather(*[
    ctx.run_node(auditor, node_input=l, use_sub_branch=True)
    for l in lines])

Two ways to start the same child.

create_task: the child dies. No log line, no error.

gather: the child's error is raised in the parent.

Rule: await every ctx.run_node call.

The third bug is the quietest. Never start a child with asyncio.create_task: the ADK source says plainly that errors are silently swallowed, in agents/context.py lines 453 to 456. The top half of the animation shows that child dying with no log line, and the parent never hears. The bottom half is the same child under gather: its error is raised in the parent, and the workflow reports the failure. The fix is the code you already have from the lab: await every ctx.run_node call, directly or through asyncio.gather. Think of create_task as sending a junior adjuster out with no phone: if the car breaks down, the office never hears. A likely question: "What if I want the other children to keep going when one fails?" Then handle it on purpose, for example with return_exceptions=True on gather, and decide in code what a failed line means; do not let the error vanish.
