This slide runs the chain from Lab 3.1 and follows the data. Press through the three paths. Path 1, node_input: parse_request returns "A-1001", and the edge gives it to check_policy. Path 2, ctx.state: check_policy writes refund_case into state, and the write_reply prompt reads it with {refund_case}. The RefundCase that also rides the edge is not what the prompt template uses. Path 3, a state-bound parameter: write_reply saves its answer under output_key reply_text, and audit_log gets it only because its parameter has the same name. Think of a kitchen: the plate moves along the pass, and the order ticket hangs on the rail where every cook can read it. A likely question: "What if I misspell the parameter?"
