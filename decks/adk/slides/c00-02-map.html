Walk the staircase from left to right. Each step up adds one layer: principles, then the ADK 2.0 mechanisms, then the parts around the model, then the newest patterns and disciplines, then production. The colour of each station is the chapter tone. The same colour means the same concept in every diagram: coral for model calls, blue for agents, violet for humans, green for state, amber for tools, red for risk. Analogy: a climbing route with fixed anchors. You clip into one anchor before you reach for the next. The hands-on thread is the official codelab "ADK 2 Orchestration: Graph, Collaborative & Dynamic Workflows". It pins ADK 2.3.0, so check the version before you run it. Likely question: "Why do the design patterns come so late?" Answer: a pattern is a choice of nodes and edges. You must know routes, HITL and context first to judge what a pattern costs. The last chapter ends with open challenges, not with a solved list.
