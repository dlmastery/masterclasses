Pinned and Still Unstable, from September 2026, reruns the same judge on the same inputs with temperature 0 and a pinned model version. About 5 percent of verdicts still flip between reruns, and on close calls, the cases near the decision line, about 40 percent flip. The tiles are schematic: four clear items with 2 flips in 40 verdicts, which is 5 percent, and one close call that flips 4 of 10 times. For the Acme gate this means two things: sample the judge 5 times per case with num_samples, and judge a change by at least three runs, with AgentEvaluator num_runs, because adk eval has no repeat flag in 2.13.0. Analogy: a bathroom scale that reads 70 or 71 kilos on the same morning; weigh three times before you celebrate. A likely question: "Is temperature 0 not deterministic?" Not in practice: the paper measured these flips with temperature 0 and a pinned model.
