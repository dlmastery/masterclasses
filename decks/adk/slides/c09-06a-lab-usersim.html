Step twelve: instead of a frozen conversation, an LLM plays the customer. A fixed golden script breaks as soon as the agent asks a clarifying question; a simulator answers it. The NOVICE persona knows its order is A-2040 but says so only when the agent asks. Think of a mystery shopper with a hidden brief: the conversation_plan holds the secret facts and the goal, and the simulator sends the finished tag when the goal is met. In this run the agent opened the ticket but also told the customer the 820 dollar refund was approved; models do not follow an instruction every time, and this is the slip the rubric must catch in step 14. The built-in personas are EXPERT, NOVICE and EVALUATOR. A likely question: "Are simulators realistic?" Not fully: research shows they over-cooperate and rarely give up like real users, so add a frustrated persona when you can.
