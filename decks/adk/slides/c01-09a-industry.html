Blog Vercel: We removed 80% of our agent's tools
Vercel's d0 agent answers data questions with SQL (post of 2025-12-22). Step 1: version 1 had 17 specialised tools; version 2 gives the model two general tools, ExecuteCommand and ExecuteSQL, and lets it grep a well-documented semantic layer of YAML, Markdown and JSON. Step 2: on their 5 test queries it went from 274.8 to 77.4 seconds, from 4 of 5 to 5 of 5 correct, from about 102k to 61k tokens, and from about 12 to 7 steps; the worst old run took 724 seconds, 100 steps and 145,463 tokens and still failed. Step 3: be honest about the caveat: five queries is an anecdote, and the trick only works when the data is documented well enough to search. Analogy: a junior with two sharp tools and a good manual beats one with a drawer of 17 gadgets. Likely question: "So should I merge the three refund desk tools?" No: keep them; add or merge tools only when an eval shows a measured gain. Next: Shopify's tool-count bands.
