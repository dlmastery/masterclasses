arXiv 2609.12191 GAUGE
GAUGE, from September 2026, ran 25 agents with simulated customers on tau-squared-bench and SimulatorArena, then compared the LLM judge's verdict with the real task outcome. Of the chats the judge rated satisfied, 57.5 percent had failed the customer's task. On close pairs, meaning two agents with similar scores, the judge and the real reward disagreed 31 percent of the time, against under 1 percent for clearly different agents. The scatter is schematic: 80 chats, 40 rated satisfied, and 23 of those 40, which is 57.5 percent, turn red because the task failed. The Monday change is a judge-free completion bit next to every rubric, which the next slides build. Analogy: a polite customer saying "thanks" is not the same as the parcel arriving. A likely question: "So should I drop the judge?" No: keep it for tone and meaning, but never let it alone decide whether the task was done.
