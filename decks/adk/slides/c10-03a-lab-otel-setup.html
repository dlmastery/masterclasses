from google.adk.telemetry.setup import maybe_set_otel_providers
# ... asyncio, os, trace, Runner, InMemorySessionService, types

os.environ.setdefault("OTEL_SERVICE_NAME", "acme-hr")
os.environ.setdefault("OTEL_RESOURCE_ATTRIBUTES",
                      "deployment.environment=dev")
os.environ.setdefault("ADK_CAPTURE_MESSAGE_CONTENT_IN_SPANS",
                      "false")          # default: on

hooks = ([google_cloud.get_gcp_exporters(enable_cloud_tracing=True)]
         if os.getenv("TO_CLOUD") else [])
maybe_set_otel_providers(otel_hooks_to_setup=hooks)

Content capture defaults to on. Set it to false yourself.

No hooks: OTLP to :4318. TO_CLOUD=1: Cloud Trace, with roles/telemetry.writer.

Set up telemetry first, then import the app from step 1.

Step two of the Acme HR lab traces the guarded app from step one, and keeps employee data out of the spans. Press once: the three setdefault lines name the service and the environment, and turn content capture off; ADK_CAPTURE_MESSAGE_CONTENT_IN_SPANS defaults to on, so without this line every prompt and reply lands in every span, and the card on the right shows the question struck out. Press again: with no hooks, maybe_set_otel_providers still adds the generic OTLP exporter from OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TRACES_ENDPOINT, for example Jaeger on port 4318; with TO_CLOUD=1 it sends OTLP over HTTP to telemetry.googleapis.com, which needs roles/telemetry.writer. Without the otel extra, export is disabled with only one warning, which is easy to miss. Press again: the app import comes after the setup lines. Analogy: a trace is a flight log many people can read; it records times and fuel, not the passengers' conversations. Likely question: "Where do I keep the full content for debugging?" In an access-controlled store, keyed by the trace id, not in the spans.
