Paper slide, August 2026. Step 0: the instruction and the output_schema both reach the model, and this schema starts with the label fields. Click 1: make the two texts conflict: the instruction says high means injury only, and the Field description says high means a loss over $10k. The paper measured accuracy drops of 5 to 45 points from conflicts like this; Claude Haiku 4.5 fell from 52.5% to 7%, and the bar shows that drop to scale. Click 2: make the two texts say the same thing and move the reasoning field to the top; a required reasoning field before the label raised schema-only accuracy by 15 to 24 points. Click 3: what to change on Monday: review Field descriptions like prompts, and keep reasoning first. Our Lab 2 ClaimIntake schema already does both. Analogy: the small print on a form is part of the instructions; if it contradicts the cover letter, people follow the small print. A likely question: "Does the field order matter to pydantic?" Not to pydantic, but the model writes the fields in order, so the reasoning is written before the label.
