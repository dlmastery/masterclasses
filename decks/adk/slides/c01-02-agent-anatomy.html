Start with the model alone. Step 1 shows the 2022 picture: one prompt that grows with every thought, action and observation until it overflows. Step 2 replaces it with the 2026 anatomy: a session log outside the window, a context policy that compiles what each call sees, tools in a sandbox, a verifier with a feedback edge, a human for escalation and a trigger that starts the run. The table maps each part to its ADK 2.0 home; students must be able to recite this mapping in an interview. Step 3 is the evidence: the same GPT-3.5 went from 48.1% to 95.1% on HumanEval inside an agent loop, above GPT-4 zero-shot at 67.0%, and AlphaCodium took GPT-4 from 19% to 44% pass@5 with a flow. Analogy: the model is the engine; the harness is the car. Likely question: "Will better models remove the harness?" Anthropic's experience says no: the harness moves. They dropped context resets and sprint splits as models improved, but kept the planner and the evaluator.
