The tool description is part of your accuracy and part of your attack surface. Step 1: in Toolflation (arXiv 2610.28454, 20 Oct 2026), seller agents write tool descriptions, and they spontaneously over-claim, so every tool says it is the best. Step 2: buyer agents stop reading and pick by list position; under one model, task success fell from 89% to about 30%. The animation is a schematic of that finding, not the paper's own figure. Step 3: the Monday change for Acme is cheap: keep each docstring specific about when to call the tool, what it returns and what it will not do, with no ranking claims, and review the descriptions of any third-party or MCP tools before you load them. Analogy: when every product on the shelf says "number one", shoppers grab the one at eye level. Likely question: "Does ADK rank tools?" No: the model reads the descriptions in the request; ADK passes them through verbatim. Next: a paper on what to do when tool interfaces drift.
