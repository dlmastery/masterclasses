One Skill Takes Over: Clashing Agent Skills and a One-File Fix

Two same-job skills drop rule compliance 93–99% → 61–62%. One merged file with one correct worked example gets 96–99%.
Lint for overlapping descriptions. Merge them into one SKILL.md.
Paper arXiv 2610.25064
The second October paper looks at what happens when two skills do the same job. Click 1: two refund skills load into one context, each with its own rules. Click 2: they do not blend; one takes over, and the other's rules disappear, so rule compliance drops from 93 to 99% down to 61 to 62% across the models tested. Click 3: the fix is one merged file. One combined SKILL.md with one correct worked example gets 96 to 99% of the achievable rules, and the takeover rate falls to 1 to 15%; most of the gain comes from the worked example, and only when that example is correct. For Acme: lint skill descriptions for overlap before you install a new skill, and merge rather than stack. Think of two managers giving one clerk different instructions for the same form: the clerk follows the louder one. A likely question: "Can I just order the skills by priority?" The paper's fix is structural: one file, one example, so there is nothing left to rank.
