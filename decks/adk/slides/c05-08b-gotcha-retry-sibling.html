The second retry bug is issue 7524, still open. The reporter's graph fans out two nodes, flaky and send_email, under a workflow that carries the retry_config. When flaky fails once on a SQLite session, the workflow retry re-runs the finished send_email too, so the customer gets two emails; a retry on the flaky node itself sends one. The cause is a race: the finished sibling's output was still being persisted when the batch was cancelled, which is why InMemorySessionService passes and SQLite fails. So test retry and resume on the session backend you use in production, not only in memory. Our payout already follows the fix, because the retry sits on pay and nowhere else. Think of a group order: if one dish is wrong, you re-cook that dish, not the whole table's order. A likely question: "Is the workflow-level retry ever right?" Only when every child is idempotent; until the issue is fixed, keep retries on leaf nodes.
