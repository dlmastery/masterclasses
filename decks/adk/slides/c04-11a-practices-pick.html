The last three slides turn the chapter into rules you can check in a design review. The first rule picks the shape, and the ladder reads left to right by cost. Start with one agent and at most 20 tools: Shopify found tool boundaries clear below 20 and blurred between 20 and 50 when they built Sidekick, and their advice is "Avoid multi-agent architectures early." Move to a graph workflow once there are more than two branching paths, which is the guidance from the Google I/O 2026 agent sessions; in a graph the model classifies and code routes. Do not default to a central multi-agent system for user-facing support: arXiv 2610.27655 tested about 7,950 conversations on tau2-bench and found a centralized multi-agent system worse than a single agent in 12 of 13 cells. Analogy: you do not hire a department for a job one person does well. A likely question: "When does multi-agent pay?" When an eval on your own tasks shows it, typically for parallel, independent research where each worker needs its own context.
