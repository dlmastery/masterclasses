Keep the $500 rule in a code metric.
arXiv 2608.26623 AgentJudgeBench
AgentJudgeBench, from August 2026, tests LLM judges on agentic tool-calling cases at several difficulty levels. Without ground truth, all six judges converge to 77 to 82 percent alignment on the hard cases, a structural ceiling. Structured rubrics help by at most 6.5 points, and chain of thought or temperature had negligible effect. The dots are schematic: they show where the six judges land, all inside the reported band. For the Acme gate this is the reason the 500 dollar rule is a code metric: a rule that must hold every time cannot sit on an instrument that is wrong about one case in five. Analogy: a referee who is right four times out of five is fine for style points, not for the goal line. A likely question: "Will a bigger judge fix it?" The paper saw the plateau across judges, so the fix is a different kind of check, not a bigger judge.
