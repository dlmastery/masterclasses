Walk the four focus steps with the $820 token. intake splits "A-2040 820" and writes amount to state. draft_reply is an LLM Agent whose output_key writes its reply to state["draft"]. The chain wires START, intake, draft_reply and manager_gate, and the gate receives amount and draft as arguments because the parameter names match the state keys. Finally the route map: approved goes to issue_refund, rejected to notify_rejected, and revise goes back to draft_reply.
