Step two of the cache lab adds the App and runs three questions in one session. ContextCacheConfig sets a 4,096-token minimum, a 15-minute TTL and cache_intervals=10, which is how many invocations reuse one cache before ADK refreshes it; the defaults are 1,800 seconds and 10. min_tokens is compared with the previous request's prompt_token_count, so turn 1 always prints cached=None. Press once: the first bar is all fresh tokens, and the dashed outline marks the 8,960-token prefix that will be cached. Press again: turns 2 and 3 read that prefix from the cache and only a few hundred fresh tokens are new; the counter goes from 0 to 2 hits. Press again: cached input tokens are cheaper, but the cache also bills storage for each hour of TTL, so the build-up slide later tunes the TTL to real pauses. Think of it like paying full price to open the binder once, then a lower price per turn, plus rent while it stays open. A likely question: "If cached stays None on turn 2, what is wrong?" The prefix is under 4,096 tokens, or something dynamic sits inside it; the gotcha slide shows the second case.
