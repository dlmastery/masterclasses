The parameter schema, not the name, decides which tool is picked

Which Metadata Matters for LLM Tool Selection in MCP?

Paper arXiv 2610.14334
The third paper asks what makes a model pick one MCP tool over another when two look alike. Step 1: it varied one metadata factor at a time. Flat, top-level parameters were chosen 93.3 to 100% of the time on a Create Issue task. Step 2: the parameter schema is the strongest lever: renaming the parameter "to" to "recipient_id" cut the selection of an otherwise equal tool to 1 to 10%. Step 3: when only the names differ, list order decides: the first-listed tool won 92 to 100% of the time. Step 4: what to change on Monday: design flat, well-named parameters, remove near-duplicate tools with tool_filter, and shuffle tool order in your evals so order does not hide a bad schema. Think of two identical doors: people take the one with the clearer handle, and if the handles match, the one nearest to them. A likely question: "Does the docstring not matter?" It does, but in this study the schema moved selection more than the description.
