The second paper is the warning label for the first. In 4,500 executions, a cheaper and faster decision model lowered the cost per attempt, but task success fell in two mobile agents. In one agent, "mean cost per successful outcome increases even as spending per attempt falls", because failed runs repeat work. Step 1: the cost per attempt line goes down. Step 2: the cost per success line goes up. The chart shows the direction only, not measured values. Step 3: so measure the number that matters: cost per resolved ticket. Sum the token counts in event.usage_metadata for each session, and divide by the sessions that end resolved. The paper also recommends preserving progress, supporting recovery and verifying completion, which Chapter 5 builds with retries and resume. Analogy: a cheap triage nurse saves money only if fewer patients come back. A likely question: "What do I report to finance?" Cost per resolved ticket and the resolve rate, side by side, never cost per call alone. Next: the chapter rules.
