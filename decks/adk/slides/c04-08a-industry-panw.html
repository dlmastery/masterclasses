Palo Alto Networks builds a Document of Record, a DOR, for pre-sales: a Salesforce trigger starts a FastAPI orchestrator on GKE, which sends a predefined list of over 140 standard questions to two ADK agents on Vertex AI Agent Engine in batches of five. Gemini 2.5 Flash rewrites each question, both agents retrieve from RAG Engine and return an answer with a groundedness score, the orchestrator keeps the better answer and labels it low, medium or high, and Pub/Sub carries the finished document back to Salesforce. The first version sent all 140 questions through one agent context and hit Out of Memory errors; sharing one GKE cluster between orchestrator and agent also gave "frequent pod crashes", so they moved to one question per call, state in the orchestrator, and separate services. The lesson for Acme: the document lives in your process, not in the agent's context. Analogy: the orchestrator is the editor who keeps the binder; each agent call is a freelancer who gets one question and a blank page. A likely question: "How fast is it now?" The post gives about 2.5 hours per DOR before optimisation and does not publish the time after it. Next: the same pattern in 16 lines of ADK code.
