Each press adds one ring, and each ring moves the attention of the engineer outward. Step 1 is prompt engineering, 2022 to 2023: the words of one call. Step 2 is flow engineering, named by the AlphaCodium paper in January 2024: the same GPT-4 went from 19% to 44% pass@5 because the sequence of steps and tests changed. Step 3 is context engineering, popular from June 2025: what each call sees. Step 4 is harness engineering, named by Mitchell Hashimoto in February 2026: every mistake of the agent triggers a permanent fix in its environment. Step 5 is the band across all rings: loop and graph engineering are the control-flow layer, and they are practice labels, not named publications. Analogy: first you tuned the sentence, then the recipe, then the ingredients on the table, then the whole kitchen. Likely question: "Which of these does ADK 2.0 target?" Mostly the control-flow band: Workflow edges, routes and bounded loops.
