Compaction keeps a long Acme chat cheap: when the prompt reaches token_threshold, ADK summarises older events and keeps the most recent raw ones. Press once: events pile up until they reach 400K tokens; Uber compacts at 400K even on 1M-context models, because quality and cost both get worse long before the window is full. Press again: the older events fold into one summary event and the last ten raw events stay, which is what event_retention_size sets. Press again: turn on the experimental telemetry features context_cache, compaction and token_usage, and the ADK telemetry spans get cache and compaction counters, so you can prove the change worked. Import EventsCompactionConfig from google.adk.apps.app; the package google.adk.apps exports only App and ResumabilityConfig. Think of compaction as minutes of a long meeting: keep the last few comments word for word and summarise the rest. A likely question: "Can I compact every N turns instead?" Yes, with the other pair, compaction_interval plus overlap_size; set one full pair, as the gotcha slide shows.
