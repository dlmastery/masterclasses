This chapter is Lab 6 on the Acme claims intake. Every night a batch of claims arrives, and it must not reach the fraud-model training table until it passes a contract. Click 1: the one valid row of the lab batch goes through. Click 2: the three bad rows go to quarantine with the failure report; nothing is deleted. Click 3: the second half of the lab. The adjusters can label only 100 claims per day, so the model picks the claims with the smallest margin, the ones it is least sure about. Analogy: the contract is the security check at the airport door, and active learning is the officer who picks the few bags worth opening by hand. Likely question: "Is this not the job of the data warehouse?" The warehouse can check too, but the training job imports the same contract class, so one definition guards both gates.
