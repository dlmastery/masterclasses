Modern LLM data is filtered hard, synthesized at scale and verified before use

From 2023 to 2026 most gains came from model-based filtering and verified synthetic data, not from more raw tokens.

This chapter covers the three middle stages of the lifecycle for LLM data: generate, select and curate. The funnel shows the main fact of the last three years: the best open datasets throw away about 90% of the web with a model-based classifier, and they still win. Analogy: a gold mine does not dig more rock to get more gold; it improves the sieve. Step 1 shows the datasets and tools we use in this chapter. Step 2 gives the October 2026 versions, and it marks distilabel as a sustainability risk because it has had no PyPI release since January 2025. A likely student question: "If we discard 90%, do we not run out of tokens?" Yes, at long horizons; Nemotron-CC answers that with rephrasing, and we see it on the next slide.
