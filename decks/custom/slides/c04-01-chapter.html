RAG gives the model fresh, private and citable knowledge at run time

Retrieval changes the tokens in the window, not the weights. It wins when knowledge is large, changing, permissioned or needs citations.

hybrid search contextual retrieval reranking Vertex AI RAG Engine agentic RAG RAFT
First check the size. If the knowledge base is “smaller than 200,000 tokens”, Anthropic suggests that you put all of it in the prompt and use prompt caching.
This chapter is about the second big lever: change what the model reads, not what it is. RAG splits documents into chunks, indexes them, and at query time pulls the few chunks that matter into the context window. An analogy: fine-tuning is studying for the exam; RAG is an open-book exam with a good librarian. Step 1 shows the toolbox for this chapter. Step 2 is the sanity check that saves weeks: under about 200k tokens, put the whole corpus in the prompt and cache it. A likely student question: "Does long context make RAG obsolete?" Answer: not for large, changing or permissioned corpora, and not when you need citations per chunk.
