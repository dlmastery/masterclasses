This is the last step of the claims-agent lab: we run slices.py and then quality_card.py, and the shell prints exit=1. The headline 83.9% looks like a release, but the gate reads the lower bound of each slice CI: health/es is 0.33 and travel/es is 0.42, both under the 0.50 gate, and the judge kappa of 0.58 is under 0.60, so three failures block the deploy job in CI. Note that auto/es passes the CI gate with only 8 items, which is why the card also lists thin slices as known gaps. The fix is not a new threshold: collect more Spanish health and travel claims, recalibrate or replace the judge, re-run, and diff the new card against this one. Analogy: the card is a unit test for the release; you do not delete a failing test, you fix the code or the data. Likely question: "Why not gate on the point estimate?" Because a thin slice can pass by luck; the lower bound makes the gate fail until there is enough evidence. Before we collect more data, the next slide checks that the eval harness counted the pass column correctly.
