Best practices

Synthetic data rules: seed real, verify cheap first, anchor humans

Generate and verify

  • Seed from real data; stratify by attributes.Else: mode collapse (2609.38359).
  • Verify in cost order: parse, check, judge.The judge sees only clean rows.
  • Reject chat-template special tokens.WebFovea: 4.9% of episodes (2610.03036).
  • Drop or slice closed-book items.They test memory, not retrieval.

Freeze sets, anchor judges

  • Freeze eval sets by hash; never train on them.Decontaminate, then adjudicate.
  • Pin the judge; keep a 200-item human anchor.Newer is not better (2610.09820).
  • Compute n_eff before adding judges.10 judges ≈ 3.5 (2609.22512).
  • Add fresh human data every round.Safe ratio is still open (2609.18878).

Don't

  • Read judge consensus as validity.α +0.069, humans flat (2610.13303).
  • Rely on one stale generator library.distilabel 1.5.3: 2025-01-28.

n_eff.py · standard design-effect formula
import numpy as np

# err[j, i] = 1 if judge j is wrong on trusted item i
def n_eff(err):
    C = np.corrcoef(err)      # judge x judge error corr.
    k = C.shape[0]
    rho = (C.sum() - k) / (k * (k - 1))   # mean off-diagonal
    return k / (1 + (k - 1) * rho)

# 10 judges, rho = 0.21 on ~100 trusted items
print(round(10 / (1 + 9 * 0.21), 2))   # 3.46
Mean off-diagonal error correlation: rho = 0.21 on ~100 trusted items.
10 judges count as 3.46. Flat n_eff? Buy human labels instead.
This is the checklist for every synthetic data job in the deck, for training data and for eval sets. The first column is about generation: seed from real data and an attribute grid, run cheap exact checks before the judge, and drop template tokens and closed-book items. The second column protects the measurement: freeze eval sets by hash, pin the judge and keep a human anchor, and remember that correlated judges are fewer judges than they look. The formula on the right is the standard design effect: with mean error correlation 0.21, ten judges carry the evidence of about 3.5 independent ones. Analogy: ten witnesses who read the same newspaper are not ten independent witnesses. Likely question: "So should I never use a judge panel?" Use one, but measure n_eff on trusted items first, and when it stops growing, spend the money on human labels.
