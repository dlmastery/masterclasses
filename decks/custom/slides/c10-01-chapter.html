Acme HR policy assistant (RAG) · Lab 9 ragas 0.4.3 · openai 3.37.0 datasketch 2.0.0 · MinHash LSH krippendorff 0.9.0 · scikit-learn 1.9.1 Docs Testset Generation for RAG
Chapter 10 is Lab 9: the Acme HR policy assistant, a RAG system over the PTO, parental-leave and expense policies. We generate a synthetic eval set with ragas, then treat every generated item as a draft: it must be grounded in its contexts, it must not be answerable without retrieval, and it must not duplicate another item. The survivors get a content hash, so the eval set is frozen and the quality card can cite it. After that we decontaminate the training data against the frozen eval set with MinHash LSH, and we correct the LLM judge's pass rate with human labels. Analogy: a factory line with three inspection gates, where only the parts that pass all three get a serial number and go into the sealed box. Likely question: "Why not trust the generator and skip the gates?" Because in the lab output 11 of the 40 generated items fail a gate, and a closed-book item measures the model's memory, not your retrieval; the animation is a schematic, the real numbers come in the lab steps.
