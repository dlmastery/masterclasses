Preference tuning

When the right answer is a judgment, train on pairs. Watch what the margin is made of.

Acme support QA: on-brand replies Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct + LoRA · DPOTrainer 3 seed pairs ×100 + 3,000 UltraFeedback pairs trl 1.16.0 · peft 0.21.2 · one 16-24 GB GPU Docs TRL DPO Trainer
This chapter is Lab 5: the Acme support QA team already marks replies as on-brand, which means empathetic, specific and taking action, or off-brand, which means dismissive or blaming the customer. We turn those judgments into chosen and rejected pairs and train with DPO plus LoRA, so there is no reward model and no sampling during training. Watch the bars: DPO pushes the two replies apart, but most of the margin comes from the rejected reply falling, not from the chosen reply rising; the numbers are the illustrative log values from the lab. Analogy: a coach who mostly says "never say that again" changes the habit fast, but can also make the player too quiet, so we read samples too. Likely question: "When do I need a reward model instead?" When you want a learned score for RL or for ranking new replies, which is the build-up later in this chapter.
