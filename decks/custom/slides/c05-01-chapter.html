05

GRPO with a verifier

GRPO: sample 8 answers per prompt, then reinforce the ones above the group average.

Acme claims payout calculator Qwen2.5-0.5B + LoRA · one 24 GB GPU verifier: math-verify
This chapter is Lab 4: the Acme claims back-office wants a small model that computes a claim payout, deductible first, then the coinsurance share, then the policy limit. Three words first. The policy is the model we train. LoRA r=16 is a small trainable adapter on top of frozen weights. math_verify is a library that reads the \boxed{} answer and compares it to the gold answer. Watch the loop: one claim prompt goes in, the policy samples 8 answers, the verifier gives each a 1 or a 0, and each reward minus the group mean, divided by the group standard deviation, becomes the advantage. That division is the TRL default, scale_rewards="group", so with 2 of 8 correct the advantages are about +1.62 and -0.54, not +0.75 and -0.25. The chart on the right is the payoff: the group mean reward climbs from 0.25 to 0.875 over five rounds, and that is the curve we want in the logs. Analogy: a teacher grades 8 attempts at the same question and tells the student which attempts beat the class average. Likely question: "Can I run this on a 16 GB Colab T4?" The lab is tested for one 24 GB GPU such as an L4, A10G or RTX 4090; a smaller card needs a shorter max_completion_length and a smaller batch.
