Lab 2 model: PEFT's default for qwen3 is q_proj, v_proj only. Lab 2 sets all-linear; keep the assert in CI.
Lab 2 was safe because we passed all-linear; this trap appears when the default targets meet a hybrid model. The bug report: a DPO-LoRA run on NVIDIA-Nemotron-Nano-9B-v2 finished 25 of 25 steps, nothing crashed, and the DPO loss sat at 0.6931 on every step. Quick DPO reminder: the loss is minus log sigmoid of beta times the margin between the policy and the reference; when the policy equals the reference the margin is zero, and the loss is ln 2. So the adapter barely changed the model. The cause: this model has 56 layers, 27 Mamba-2, 25 MLP and only 4 attention, and PEFT's default targets for nemotron_h are attention projections. PEFT raises only when nothing matches, so a partial match is silent. Partial coverage does not always pin the loss exactly at ln 2; here the 4 attention layers were too small a share of the model to move it in 25 steps. The fix: target all-linear, which adds the Mamba in_proj and out_proj and the MLP up and down projections, and assert one LoRA layer per decoder layer before training. For the Lab 2 model the same lesson holds: PEFT's default for qwen3 is q_proj and v_proj only, which on Qwen3-0.6B gives 2.3 million instead of 10.1 million trainable parameters. A related load-time trap: PEFT drops unexpected adapter keys without a warning, and strict=True is ignored (PEFT #3804), so diff the adapter keys against the model keys and check that the adapter answer differs from the disable_adapter answer on 5 notes. Think of a dimmer switch wired to 4 bulbs out of 56: the switch moves, the room stays dark.
