DPO turns preference learning into a classification loss on chosen and rejected pairs

LDPO = −E[ log σ( βKL strength ( log πθ(y+|x)πref(y+|x)chosen: push up − log πθ(y−|x)πref(y−|x)rejected: push down ) ) ]

The closed-form optimal policy of the KL-regularized RLHF objective turns RLHF into a logistic loss. DPO “eliminat[es] the need for sampling from the LM during fine-tuning”.

DPO works “by suppressing the likelihood of dispreferred completions rather than by increasing the likelihood of preferred ones” (TRL docs). So logps/chosen can fall. Watch rewards/margins and an eval.

dpo_lora_mpo.py · TRL DPO docs, reflowed

from trl import DPOConfig, DPOTrainer
from peft import LoraConfig
from datasets import load_dataset

args = DPOConfig(
    beta=0.1, learning_rate=1e-5,  # higher LR for adapters
    loss_type=["sigmoid", "bco_pair", "sft"],  # MPO
    loss_weights=[0.8, 0.2, 1.0])
ds = load_dataset("trl-lib/ultrafeedback_binarized",
                  split="train")
trainer = DPOTrainer("Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B", args=args,
                     train_dataset=ds,
                     peft_config=LoraConfig())
trainer.train()
  • Defaults: beta 0.1, learning_rate 1e-6, max_length 1024.
  • loss_type is a list. Options include hinge, ipo, robust, bco_pair, apo_zero, apo_down, discopop, sft, sigmoid_norm (SimPO-style).
  • ref_model=None uses the initial policy.
  • sync_ref_model=True gives TR-DPO. label_smoothing gives Robust DPO.

Read the loss from the inside out. The first log-ratio measures how much more likely the chosen answer is under the policy than under the reference; the second does the same for the rejected answer; beta scales the gap and acts as the strength of the implicit KL constraint. There is no reward model and no sampling: it is a binary classifier on pairs. Then place the relatives on the 2x2 grid: do you need a reference model, and do you have pairs or only thumbs up and down. Analogy: DPO is a referee who only says "A beat B", and the model learns to widen the gap. Likely question: "Why do chosen log-probs drop during DPO?" Answer: the loss only needs the margin to grow, and pushing the rejected answer down is the easiest way; add an SFT term (MPO) to anchor the chosen answers.
