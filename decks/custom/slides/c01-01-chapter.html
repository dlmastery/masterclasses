Choose the cheapest lever that fixes the failure

Prompt, retrieve, tune or reinforce. Each rung up the ladder costs more data, more money and more platform risk.

context engineering RAG SFT DPO RFT / GRPO

“you use the right optimization tool for the right job”

Doc OpenAI, Optimizing LLM accuracy
This chapter answers one question: which lever do I pull first? The ladder on the right goes from the cheapest lever, prompt and context, to the most expensive, reinforcement learning. Blue rungs change only the tokens in the window; coral rungs change the weights. The amber bars show that cost, data needs and platform risk grow with each rung. Analogy: you do not rebuild an engine when the driver only needs a better map. Likely question: "Is RL always the best result?" No. RL needs a grader and the most compute; use it only when the cheaper rungs fail the eval.
