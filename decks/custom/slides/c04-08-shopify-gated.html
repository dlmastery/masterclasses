Blog Shopify Engineering: Building production-ready agentic systems
Shopify Sidekick is the clearest public case of eval first, training second. Before any GRPO, the team sampled ground-truth sets from real merchant conversations, had at least three product experts label each one, and iterated the judge prompt until its Cohen kappa against the experts rose from 0.02 to 0.61, close to the human-human ceiling of 0.69. Only then did they train, with a gated reward: cheap procedural checks on syntax and schema run first, and only a rollout that passes them reaches the LLM judge. Even so, the policy found opt-out answers, tag hacking and hallucinated IDs, and fixing those moved syntax validity from about 93% to about 99%. Analogy: you calibrate the scale before you weigh the gold. Likely question: "Why not let the judge check syntax too?" The procedural gate is cheap, exact and cannot be argued with, so the expensive judge only scores answers that are already valid, exactly as the REQUIRED gate in Lab 3 runs before any decision score.
