Reasoning-Preserving Fine-Tuning of Post-RL LLMs with Null-Basis LoRA

Plain LoRA SFT drops reasoning 63.8% → 8.3% (Qwen2.5-3B) and 80.1% → 9.4% (1.5B). NB-LoRA trains ΔW = B·C·NsT in the null space of reasoning activations: same task accuracy, reasoning near pre-FT, 6.3x faster than NSCL.
When you SFT a thinking model like Qwen3, re-run a reasoning benchmark on both arms.
arXiv:2607.23711 26 Jul 2026

The Intruder Threshold: A Spectral Law for LoRA Fine-Tuning

Predicts which LoRA layers grow forgetting-driving intruder dimensions. Mistral-7B r256: forgetting +4.71 → +1.77 WikiText-2 PPL. A uniform lower alpha, or half the LR, works about as well.
If you see forgetting, lower alpha or LR first.

How Much Can a LoRA Adapter Memorize?

LoRA stores 2–3.4 bits per parameter. At equal accuracy, SFT memorizes ~3x more example-specific information than GRPO.
Add a general-capability regression to lab4_eval_before_after.py.
arXiv 2609.25618 arXiv 2607.23711 arXiv 2607.21351
Chapter 3 fine-tunes a Qwen3 model, so ask what the update damages, not only what it teaches. NB-LoRA shows the risk on post-RL models: plain LoRA SFT took reasoning from 63.8% to 8.3% on Qwen2.5-3B, because the update overlaps the directions the reasoning activations use. The fix collects about 512 reasoning rollouts, takes the null space of each layer's inputs, and trains the update only there, so the reasoning inputs see no change; picture painting a wall with tape over the window. The Intruder Threshold paper adds the cheap lesson: if you see forgetting, a lower alpha or half the learning rate works about as well as its per-layer rule. A likely question is whether this matters for privacy: the memorization paper finds SFT stores about 3x more example-specific information than GRPO at equal accuracy, so keep a general-capability regression in the before/after script.
