Chapter 11 is Lab 10: the Acme claims-extraction agent is about to ship, and we decide whether it may. We report the pass rate per line of business and language with honest confidence intervals, we compare v1 and v2 on the same 400 golden cases with a paired test, and we write a versioned quality card that a CI job checks before release. The animation is the whole chapter in one loop: the 84% headline splits into eight slices, two Spanish slices fall below the gate, the card fails, and only a fix and a re-run turn it green. Analogy: a lab report without sample size and error bars is a rumour, not a result. Likely question: "Is 84% not good enough to ship?" The headline is fine, but one slice of real customers gets 56%, and the card makes that visible before the release, not after.
