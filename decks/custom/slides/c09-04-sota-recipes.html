Every open recipe of 2025 and 2026 runs the same line. Step 1: the packet goes from pretraining to the card. Mid-training on math, code and long context sits between pretraining and SFT. SFT data is mostly distilled from a stronger teacher, preference tuning uses strong-versus-weak pairs, and RL uses verifiers as the reward. Step 2 names who made each station known: Tulu 3 named RLVR, SmolLM3 published APO pairs and a linear merge, DeepSeek-R1 went RL-first, Gemma distills every model, and OLMo 3 adds OlmoTrace. Step 3 shows how hard the data is cut: about 9.3 trillion tokens in the pool become 5.9 trillion in the mix, and mid-training uses only 100 billion. Step 4 shows what this gives in the open: MATH 96.1 for Olmo 3-Think 32B. Analogy: a car plant; every brand uses the same stations, and the difference is in the parts and the checks. Likely question: "Why merge at the end?" SmolLM3 mixed 10% of a mid-training checkpoint back in to recover long-context scores that APO had hurt.
