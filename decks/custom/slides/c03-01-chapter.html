Reinforcement learning optimizes the policy against a reward, not against gold answers

Three eras: RLHF with PPO (2022-23), direct preference optimization (2023-24), verifiable rewards with GRPO-family algorithms (2024-26). The 2026 frontier is agentic, multi-turn, tool-using RL with async rollouts.

All methods in this chapter run one loop. The policy samples answers to prompts, a reward scores them, an advantage says which answers were better than expected, and a clipped policy-gradient step moves the policy. The eras differ only in the reward source and in the way they compute the advantage. Analogy: SFT is copying a worked solution; RL is practice with a grader who only gives a score. Likely question: "Why not just do SFT on the best answers?" Answer: SFT needs gold outputs; RL needs only a reward, and it learns from its own samples, including the bad ones.
