Chapter 9 is Lab 8: the Acme triage model sorts support tickets into billing, login, outage and shipping, and we build three gates around it. First, a weekly Evidently report exits 1 and holds the retraining job when the ticket mix shifts, as it does in week 42 during a login outage. Second, cleanlab ranks the vendor's roughly 8% wrong labels, so a team lead reviews about 200 tickets instead of 3,000. Third, the router auto-routes only when P(correct) beats the 0.8 break-even of the costs 0, 1 and 5, and defers the rest to a human. Analogy: a hospital triage desk checks the intake form, re-checks suspicious charts, and calls a doctor when the nurse is not sure. Likely question: "Why not just retrain every week?" Because retraining on a shifted mix with noisy labels bakes both problems into the next model.
