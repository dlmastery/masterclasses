args = DPOConfig(
    output_dir="support-dpo",
    model_init_kwargs={"dtype": "bfloat16"},
    beta=0.1,                   # KL strength: higher = stay closer to the start model
    loss_type=["sigmoid", "sft"], loss_weights=[1.0, 0.2],  # DPO + NLL on chosen (RPO-style)
    learning_rate=5e-6,         # LoRA; full FT default is 1e-6
    per_device_train_batch_size=4, gradient_accumulation_steps=4,
    max_length=1024, num_train_epochs=1,
    eval_strategy="steps", eval_steps=50, logging_steps=10, report_to="trackio",
)
trainer = DPOTrainer(
    model="Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct",  # no ref_model: reference = adapter off
    args=args, train_dataset=split["train"], eval_dataset=split["test"],
    peft_config=LoraConfig(r=16, lora_alpha=32, target_modules="all-linear",
                           task_type="CAUSAL_LM"),
)
trainer.train()
trainer.save_model()

This is the trainer half of Lab 5: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct with a LoRA adapter, trained by DPOTrainer in TRL 1.16.0 on the pairs from step 1. Step 1: beta is the strength of the KL leash to the reference; 0.1 is the default, and a higher value keeps the policy closer to the start model. Step 2: in TRL 1.x loss_type is a list, so sigmoid DPO plus an SFT term on the chosen reply is one line, and the two lists must have the same length or DPOConfig raises the error you see. Step 3: we pass no ref_model, because with PEFT the reference is the same base model with the adapter switched off, so there is only one model in memory. Analogy: beta is the length of a dog's leash, and the reference is the post it is tied to. Likely question: why 5e-6 and not the 1e-6 default? A LoRA adapter usually needs several times more, and the October 2026 hyperparameter-landscape paper says the best LR moves with model size and does not transfer across datasets, so sweep LR and beta with short runs scored on your own eval.
