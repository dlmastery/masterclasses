Open with the claim: all teams use the same foundation models, so the model is not the advantage. The advantage comes from three levers. You change the weights with SFT, DPO and RL. You change the context with RAG and context engineering. Under both levers sits the data lifecycle: design, generate, select, curate, label, denoise and the quality card. Point at the three numbers. A 1.3B InstructGPT model beat 175B GPT-3 on human preference. Cursor got 28% more accepts with 21% fewer suggestions from online RL. Anthropic cut retrieval failures by 67%.
