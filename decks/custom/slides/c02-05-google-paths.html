Gemini lane: Vertex AI supervised tuning

Models: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.5 Flash, 3.1 Flash-Lite, 2.5 Pro, 2.5 Flash-Lite, 2.5 Flash. Data: JSONL in Cloud Storage, “at least 100 to 500 examples”. Knobs: epoch_count, adapter_size, learning_rate_multiplier (default 1). Run the first job with the defaults. For tuned tasks, turn thinking off or use the minimum budget.

vertex_tune.py · google-genai SDK (adapted)

import time
from google import genai
from google.genai import types
client = genai.Client(http_options=types.HttpOptions(api_version="v1"))
job = client.tunings.tune(
    base_model="gemini-3.5-flash",
    training_dataset=types.TuningDataset(gcs_uri="gs://b/tune.jsonl"),
    config=types.CreateTuningJobConfig(
        tuned_model_display_name="support-tone-v1", epoch_count=1))
done = {"JOB_STATE_SUCCEEDED", "JOB_STATE_FAILED", "JOB_STATE_CANCELLED"}
while job.state not in done:
    time.sleep(60); job = client.tunings.get(name=job.name)
resp = client.models.generate_content(
    model=job.tuned_model.endpoint, contents="why is the sky blue?")
The Gemini API and AI Studio have no tuning. Tune Gemini on Vertex AI, or tune open Gemma weights yourself.

Google gives two lanes. Step 1, the Gemma lane: you own the weights; you train with QLoRA in Colab or Kaggle using TRL or Unsloth, then merge, quantize and serve on Cloud Run with vLLM or on the device with LiteRT or WebGPU. The four starting points in the table are real, current Google and Unsloth materials. Step 2, the Gemini lane: you do not get the weights; you upload JSONL to Cloud Storage and Vertex AI trains an adapter and gives you an endpoint. Step 3 and 4: the whole managed job is one tune call and a polling loop. Step 5: remember that the Gemini API and AI Studio removed tuning, so tuning Gemini means Vertex. Analogy: Gemma is buying the car and servicing it yourself; Gemini on Vertex is a lease with a service plan. Likely question: "Which lane should a startup choose?" Choose Gemma if you need on-device, full control or portability; choose Vertex if you want Gemini quality and no GPU operations.
