This is the whole course on one page. Acme is the thread: the Acme refund agent comes back in Labs 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9, and each other business appears only where its problem needs that lab's feature. Analogy: it is a metro line with ten stations (labs) through four neighbourhoods (businesses), and Acme is the line you ride end to end. Press once: the line draws with ten stations and the table appears faded. Press again: the Acme rows light up in blue; again: the Northwind rows in green; again: Bluefin in amber and Contoso in violet. The next three presses build where the code runs, from the bottom up: ADK 2.0 on your laptop for Labs 1 to 9; then Agent Runtime, which Lab 8 calls for Memory Bank and Lab 10 deploys to with adk deploy agent_engine; then the Gemini Enterprise app, where Lab 10 publishes the agent to employees with agents-cli. Docs use both the old and new names (Agent Runtime was Vertex AI Agent Engine); the CLI still says agent_engine. Likely question: "Do I need a cloud account?" Only for Labs 8 and 10. Next: Chapter 1 opens Lab 1, the Acme refund desk, on your laptop.
