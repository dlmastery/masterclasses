Your first agent

Build the Acme refund desk: two tools, a runner, a refund cap and a human approval gate.

refund_agent · LlmAgent lookup_order + check_refund_eligibility callbacks · refund cap + PII FunctionTool(require_confirmation=...) tested on google-adk 2.13.0
This chapter builds one agent end to end: the Acme Retail refund desk that answers "Can I return order A-1002?". We start with an LlmAgent on gemini-3.5-flash and two typed Python functions, lookup_order and check_refund_eligibility, then drive it from adk run, adk web and a Python runner with full trajectory logs. Next we move policy out of the prompt: callbacks enforce the refund cap and redact PII, and FunctionTool with require_confirmation stops refunds over $200 until a human says yes. Analogy: the instruction is the job description, the tools are the forms the clerk can fill, and the callbacks are the till that refuses a refund over the limit no matter what the clerk was told. Likely question: "Why not just write the cap in the prompt?" Because a prompt can be talked around, and a callback is code that runs every time. Everything in this chapter is tested on google-adk 2.13.0. Lab 1 starts on the next slide with the two tools.
