The router is the simplest agentic RAG: one LLM call that returns structured output and picks a source. Step one: "refund policy?" goes to the vector DB of product docs. Step two: "orders last week?" goes to text-to-SQL over the orders database. The enum in the schema stops the model from inventing a source. Adaptive-RAG (Jeong et al., NAACL 2024) adds a second decision: how much retrieval. A small T5-size classifier sends simple questions to the LLM alone, moderate ones to one retrieval, and complex ones to a multi-step IRCoT-style loop; the training labels come from which strategy got the answer right. Analogy: a hospital triage desk sends a cold to the pharmacy and a fracture to surgery. Self-Route (arXiv 2407.16833) uses the same idea to choose between RAG and long context. Likely question: "How do you evaluate a router?" Keep a labeled set of questions with the correct source and measure routing accuracy apart from answer quality.
