Retrieval quality and context assembly

Hybrid search gives recall, reranking gives precision, and context assembly decides use.

Hybrid search + RRF Rerankers Query transforms + self-query Lost in the middle / context rot Long context vs RAG + caching Context engineering
This chapter follows one query through the retrieval funnel. The first band is recall: cheap retrievers (BM25, dense and learned sparse) search millions of documents, metadata filters run inside this stage, and RRF fuses the lists into about 100 to 150 candidates. The second band is precision: a reranker sees the query and each document together and keeps 5 to 20. The third band is use: the order, the number of distractors and the total length decide whether the model actually uses what we found. Analogy: a hiring funnel, where a CV keyword screen gives recall, an interview gives precision, and the final brief to the manager decides what gets read. A likely question: "Which stage should I improve first?" Measure first; a miss at the recall stage can never be fixed later, so check recall@100 before you tune the reranker.
