First principles

An agent is a model inside a harness, not a prompt in a loop.

The loop is still a loop. What changed is everything around it.

  • What is an agent in 2026?
  • When is an agent worth its cost, compared with a workflow?
  • Which engineering disciplines replaced prompt tuning?
This chapter sets the vocabulary for the whole course. Press once: the coral ring is the agent loop, the same loop that ReAct made famous in 2022. Press again: three outer rings grow around it, for tools, context and verification. In 2026 most of the engineering work happens in those outer rings, not in the loop and not in the prompt. Analogy: a race car engine has not changed much, but the car around it (brakes, sensors, pit crew) decides who wins. Likely student question: "So is prompt engineering dead?" No. It is now the innermost ring of a larger system, and slide 4 of this chapter shows the rings that grew around it.
