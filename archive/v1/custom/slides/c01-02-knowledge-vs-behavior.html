Knowledge failures go to retrieval; behavior failures go to weights

“RAG only solves our in-context learning issues”

Doc OpenAI, Optimizing LLM accuracy Paper Ovadia et al. 2023 Paper Gekhman et al. 2024 Paper Balaguer et al. 2024

Knowledge → retrieval

Missing, stale or private facts. Use prompting and RAG.

Ovadia 2023: “RAG consistently outperforms” unsupervised fine-tuning for knowledge injection.

Gekhman 2024: models learn new facts slowly, and then hallucinate more.

Behavior → weights

Format, tone, style and consistent reasoning. Use fine-tuning, preference tuning or RL.

Balaguer 2024 (agriculture): fine-tuning gave +6 p.p. RAG added +5 p.p. on top. The two levers combine.

Do not add retrieval to a style task: BLEU fell from 87 to 83. Classify the failure before you pick the tool.
This is the OpenAI two-axis frame. The x-axis is context optimization: what the model knows. The y-axis is LLM optimization: how the model behaves. Walk the path: start with a prompt, add RAG for missing facts, fine-tune for behavior, and combine both only when the eval shows both failures. Analogy: RAG gives the model an open book; fine-tuning trains its handwriting. The Icelandic case is the key number: fine-tuning took GPT-4 from 62 to 87 BLEU, and RAG then pulled it down to 83, because grammar correction is a behavior task. Likely question: "Can I fine-tune facts into the model?" Gekhman 2024 shows that new facts are learned slowly and raise hallucination, so keep facts in retrieval.
