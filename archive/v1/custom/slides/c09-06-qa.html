Interview: the end-to-end customization system

Design an assistant for insurance-claims agents. Policies change monthly, answers must cite, and the tone must follow brand rules.
knowledge behavior data
Put the policies in RAG over versioned documents, with hybrid search, reranking and a citation check. Fix the tone with SFT or DPO. Write a data contract and a taxonomy of claim types. Build the golden set from real traces, with a slice per product line. Validate the judge against experts. Monitor query-vs-corpus drift and ship a quality card per release.
A paper claims +20 points on MATH-500 from RL on Qwen2.5-Math. How do you check it?
inflated gains
Run a random-reward control: on Qwen2.5-Math-7B, random rewards gave +21.4 and true rewards +29.1. Repeat on other model families. Decontaminate the eval. Report pass@k for large k, where base models often win. Compare on the same items with paired CIs.
How do you limit reward hacking in agentic coding RL?
reward hacking
Use hidden tests and make test files read-only. Run every rollout in a sandbox. Audit a sample of high-reward episodes. Monitor the CoT, but keep the monitor out of the reward, or the hack goes hidden. Add inoculation prompting. Stop training at the peak of the gold metric.
Your platform deprecates your tuned model next quarter. What do you keep?
platform risk
Keep the data with its contract and card, the grader, the eval set and the pinned training recipe. Then re-tune on Vertex AI, Bedrock or open weights, and compare the new model with paired CIs on the same frozen eval set.

Q3 tests reward-hacking defense in depth, and the key point is that a monitor inside the reward teaches the model to hide. Q4 is the platform question of 2026: OpenAI and Fireworks closed managed tuning paths, so only portable assets survive.
