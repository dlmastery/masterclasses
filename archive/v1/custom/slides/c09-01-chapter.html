Blog Ai2, Olmo 3 (allenai.org/blog/olmo3)
This is the last chapter. It joins the parts of the course into one workflow and then lists what is still not solved. The animation shows the seven stages of chapters 5 to 8 in a line, and then the line closes into a ring: the quality card of one release feeds the design of the next release. Ai2 calls this "the model flow" and releases every stage, checkpoint and dataset of OLMo 3. Analogy: a factory does not stop at the shipping dock; returns and complaints go back to the design office. The red badge in the centre is the second half of the chapter: reward hacking, inflated RL gains, collapse, contamination, licensing and platform risk. Likely question: "Is this workflow only for labs that pretrain?" No. The same loop runs for a 2,000-row SFT set or a RAG golden set.
